In preparation

Integrated transcriptomic analysis for host-directed target discovery in measles virus infection

K. N. Hasan, S. A. Redhila, G. M. Rahman

Undergraduate thesis research · View related project ↗

Project scope & current status

Investigates host responses to measles virus infection using public bulk and single-cell RNA-seq datasets, with differential expression, functional enrichment, cross-lineage comparison, protein–protein interaction analysis, and exploratory drug-repurposing work. The revised candidate-gene analysis is still being developed; downstream network and docking results require completion and validation before any conclusions are presented as final.
