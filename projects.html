Host-directed target discovery in measles virus infection

Integrated bulk and single-cell RNA-seq analyses to investigate persistent host-response signatures, distinguish infected-cell from bystander-cell responses, and prioritize candidates for further investigation.

Longitudinal bulk RNA-seq Single-cell RNA-seq Cross-dataset analysis

  • Investigating longitudinal host-response patterns in bulk RNA-seq dataset GSE229685.
  • Examining cell states and infected/bystander-cell context in single-cell RNA-seq dataset GSE168775.
  • Designing cross-dataset comparisons to assess evidence convergence and direction consistency.

UNDERGRADUATE RESEARCH · MANUSCRIPT IN PREPARATION

Hydrogel-based wound-dressing research

Development and optimization of physically cross-linked chitosan/PVA/gelatin composite hydrogels for antibiotic-loaded wound dressings.

My role, methods & evaluation
  • Primarily prepared the hydrogels and worked on formulation development using planned polymer blend ratios.
  • Applied physical freeze–thaw crosslinking and freeze-drying as part of the preparation workflow.
  • Participated in selected characterization tests. The project considered swelling, water retention, gel fraction, porosity, degradation, tensile properties, and additional evaluations where data were available.
  • Manuscript is in preparation. No performance outcomes are claimed here without verified results.
