RESEARCH INTERESTS

Research direction

My current thesis investigates host responses to measles virus infection using independent bulk and single-cell RNA-seq datasets, aiming to identify persistent transcriptional signatures and prioritize candidates for further investigation. Alongside computational work, I have primarily prepared chitosan/PVA/gelatin composite hydrogels for antibiotic-loaded wound-dressing research and participated in selected characterization tests. I have also contributed to collaborative CT-based lung cancer and liver tumor imaging studies. My broader goal is to integrate computational methods and biomedical engineering to address meaningful healthcare challenges.
