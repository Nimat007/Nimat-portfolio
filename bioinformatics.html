UNDERGRADUATE THESIS · COMPUTATIONAL BIOLOGY

Host-directed target discovery in measles virus infection

My initial motivation came from the 2026 measles outbreak in Bangladesh and the question of whether host-response biology could offer complementary research directions beyond vaccination. I am building a target-blind, reproducible analysis of public bulk and single-cell RNA-seq data to investigate persistent host responses, bystander-cell signatures, and candidates for further study. This is computational hypothesis generation, not evidence of a validated treatment.

THESIS OVERVIEW

Research pipeline

Integrated Transcriptomic Analysis for Host-Directed Target Discovery in Measles Virus Infection

Research question: Which host-response signatures are persistent over infection time and independently supported by single-cell evidence, including in uninfected bystander cells?

Datasets: GSE229685 (bulk RNA-seq) and GSE168775 (single-cell RNA-seq).

Current status: The revised candidate-discovery analysis is in progress. Downstream network and docking work will follow the final, supported candidate set; earlier results are not treated as final.

01 Research motivation & biological hypothesis Why this question matters and what I set out to investigate
  • Purpose: investigate host biology that may complement prevention-focused approaches.
  • Approach: discover candidates from data first; treat therapeutic relevance as a hypothesis requiring validation.

Initial idea

The 2026 measles outbreak in Bangladesh prompted me to think about the gap between preventing infection through vaccination and the need for better understanding of disease biology after infection. I became interested in whether the host response to measles could reveal biological processes worth investigating as complementary host-directed therapeutic research.

Research intention

Rather than beginning with a preferred gene or drug, I redesigned the analysis to identify candidates from the data first. The central question is whether persistent bulk-transcriptomic responses converge with cell-resolved evidence and whether some signatures are detectable in uninfected bystander cells.

Interpretation boundary

Transcriptomic association can prioritize hypotheses, but cannot establish that a gene is causal or that targeting it will treat measles. Network analysis and docking, once completed, will remain exploratory and require independent validation.

02 Longitudinal bulk RNA-seq discovery GSE229685 · temporal host-response patterns
  • What I did: analyzed the time course in bulk RNA-seq dataset GSE229685.
  • Why: identify host-response changes that persist across multiple post-infection time points.

Research question

Which genes show statistically supported and directionally persistent expression changes across post-infection time points?

Analysis plan

  • Audit sample metadata, donor/time-point structure, count-matrix alignment, and missing observations before modeling.
  • Filter low-expression genes using the prespecified count threshold.
  • Use DESeq2 for a donor-adjusted longitudinal model and a likelihood-ratio test for temporal effects.
  • Estimate time-specific post-infection versus baseline contrasts and apply effect-size shrinkage where appropriate.
  • Define persistent responses using prespecified significance and direction-consistency criteria rather than selecting genes after viewing results.

Evidence to present

Study-design timeline, PCA and QC plots, temporal expression heatmap, contrast tables, and persistent-gene trajectories will be linked or shown when the revised outputs are checked and ready. No older candidate list is presented as final.

03 Single-cell analysis of infected and bystander cells GSE168775 · cell state and epithelial context
  • What I did: examined cell-level context in single-cell RNA-seq dataset GSE168775.
  • Why: compare mock, infected, and uninfected bystander cells while respecting library-level replication limits.

Research question

Which cell populations show host-response changes, and are any responses detectable in GFP-negative bystander cells relative to mock and infected cells?

Analysis plan

  • Audit library metadata, viral-feature annotations, viral UMI distributions, and infection-state labels.
  • Apply library-aware quality control, feature/count thresholds, outlier checks, and doublet screening.
  • Compare integration approaches and inspect whether integration changes meaningful biological structure.
  • Annotate eligible epithelial populations and evaluate annotation confidence.
  • Compare bystander versus mock and infected versus bystander states within eligible lineages.
  • Use pseudobulk aggregation at the available sample/library level rather than treating individual cells as independent biological replicates.

Design limitation

Donors were pooled before sequencing in the single-cell dataset, so donor-level replication is unavailable. Cell-resolved patterns can support biological context, but inferential strength is limited by the experimental design.

Evidence to present

QC summary, UMAP with cell identities, infection-state composition, viral-feature checks, and differential-expression summaries will be added after final verification.

04 Cross-dataset convergence & robustness Independent evidence, direction agreement, sensitivity checks
  • What I did: designed cross-dataset comparison and sensitivity checks.
  • Why: prioritize only candidates supported by independent evidence and consistent direction of effect.

Research question

Which candidates have consistent support across independent bulk and single-cell analyses, and how sensitive are they to analytical choices?

Analysis plan

  • Harmonize gene identifiers and document mapping failures or duplicates.
  • Compare persistent bulk-response genes with eligible single-cell results.
  • Separate directionally concordant candidates from discordant results and document the supporting lineages.
  • Assess sensitivity to quality-control thresholds, cell annotations, integration behavior, pseudobulk eligibility, and individual libraries where estimable.
  • Advance candidates only when they meet the predefined evidence criteria.

Current interpretation

The revised analysis is intended to prevent early gene selection from steering downstream work. Candidate tables and figures will be added only when the revised code and outputs are verified. A weak association with viral burden alone would not prove a paracrine mechanism or interferon independence.

05 Network biology & structural prioritization Planned downstream work · Cytoscape, cytoHubba, PyRx

Research intention

After the unbiased candidate set is finalized, investigate whether supported genes form interpretable interaction modules and whether any encoded proteins merit exploratory structural analysis.

Planned workflow

  • Map supported genes to protein-protein associations and inspect network context in Cytoscape.
  • Use cytoHubba or related network metrics as prioritization aids, not proof of causal importance.
  • Examine functional enrichment and connected modules with appropriate caution.
  • Select protein structures and compounds only after documenting biological rationale, structure quality, binding-site definition, and preparation steps.
  • Use PyRx docking to generate hypotheses about possible protein-ligand poses; predicted scores do not establish binding, efficacy, or safety.

Status: planned/in progress. Previous network and docking outputs based on an earlier, incorrect candidate set are not presented as final evidence.
